# Code of conduct

Lucid is a small open-source project. Everyone interacting in its issues, pull requests,
discussions and other channels is expected to keep the conversation respectful and on topic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before escalating.
- Respect differing experience levels, platforms and use cases.
- Keep reports and pull requests focused and reproducible.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks unrelated to the project.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports can be sent privately to the maintainers through the
repository's **Report a vulnerability** channel or by contacting a maintainer directly; reports are
handled confidentially.

This policy is intentionally short and applies to project spaces and to public interactions made on
the project's behalf.
